wabridge

The WhatsApp bridge, rebuilt 15 September 2026 after WhatsApp stopped accepting the older client version (Client outdated (405)). WhatsApp-side change; the pairing survives; no QR scan needed.

One command, run by the assistant

Mac:

curl -fsSL https://stevenlava.com/upgrades/wabridge/install.sh | bash

Windows (PowerShell):

irm https://stevenlava.com/upgrades/wabridge/install.ps1 | iex

It finds the current bridge, backs it up, swaps in the new binary, restarts the service and waits for Connected to WhatsApp.
